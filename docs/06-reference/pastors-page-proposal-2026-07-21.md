## Clear separation from Church Partnership

- **Pastors page:** training, formation, sending, pastor stories, pastor-training support, pastor inquiry.
- **Church Partnership page:** how a church or foundation partners financially, relationally, through visits, resources, and advocacy.

## Information that should not be invented

- Who qualifies as a pastor candidate.
- Whether Favor ordains, commissions, employs, sponsors, or only trains candidates.
- Training length, calendar, tuition/cost, accommodation, or travel support.
- Exact current sequence between PBS, GIFT, FISH, and other training.
- Whether a donor can choose a “Pastor Training” designation in Blackbaud.
- The secure inquiry recipient and expected response time.
- Whether the page is public or password-protected.
