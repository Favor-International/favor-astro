## Important distinction

The current homepage publishes newer annual highlights, including 2025 figures, while Our Story presents a historical cumulative summary. Those two displays answer different questions and should not be merged.

## Recommendation

The redesigned section can remain because each claim has a direct Favor-published source. Before calling these figures “audited,” “verified totals,” or attaching a current as-of date, Favor should supply the internal cumulative report or approved impact sheet behind them. Until then, the safest editorial label is **Published by Favor on the current Our Story page**.
